# Candidates — Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line

Competition: `u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3`
($2 escrowed, top 2 paid; entry free; deadline 26 Nov 2026 18:35 WIB.)

Judge criteria, most important first:

1. Catches a small moment of wonder in everyday life in a single sentence.
2. Turns the moment at an angle the reader has not met before, so the phrasing itself is the surprise.
3. Lands its surprise in one read, staying a single clear sentence.
4. Grounds the surprise in something real and specific, not floating free of the everyday.
5. Keeps every word working, with no filler and no worn phrase.

All ten candidates are original, one sentence each, and under 200 characters.
None copies the kit's examples or any other entrant's text.

## Why #1 wins

It is the only candidate that is strong on all five criteria at once. The moment is tiny and universal — glancing at a lift mirror. The angle is unmet: the mirror does not just reflect, it *remembers*, and for half a second it shows a stranger — then the doubt turns back on the speaker in the final clause ("which of us it believed"), so the ordinary tilts into something strange in a single read. It is grounded in hard specifics (elevator, mirror, half a second, the ride), and no word is filler: every clause either sets the scene or delivers the turn.
